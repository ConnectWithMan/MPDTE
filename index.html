Overview of MP DTE Counselling

Get to know the online admission mechanism for professional graduation courses in Madhya Pradesh.

The Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Madhya Pradesh conducts the centralized MP DTE Counselling for admissions into various undergraduate engineering (B.E./B.Tech) and architecture (B.Arch) programs across the state. This online counselling process is the primary gateway for securing a seat in top government, government-aided, and private self-financing technical institutions in Madhya Pradesh.

Admissions are granted based on the merit of the candidate's performance in the JEE Main examination. The MP DTE Counselling process involves several key stages: online registration, fee payment, choice filling, document verification, and seat allotment. It is designed to be a transparent, merit-based, and efficient method for students to choose branches and colleges matching their ranks. The process caters to Madhya Pradesh domicile candidates (Home State Quota) as well as students from other states (All India Quota).

Important Note for Aspiring Students
For Government-aided and Government colleges, up to 95% of total seats are reserved for MP Domicile students. Out-of-state students can compete through the 5% All India Quota based on JEE Main CRL Rank.

Eligibility Criteria for MP DTE 2026

Make sure you satisfy these academic and entrance exam criteria before registering.

For B.E. / B.Tech Courses

  • Academic Qualification: Class 12 (or equivalent) passed with Physics, Mathematics, and one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology.
  • Minimum PCM Aggregate Marks: 45% marks for General/Unreserved candidates (40% for SC/ST/OBC candidates of Madhya Pradesh).
  • Compulsory Scorecard: Must hold a valid JEE Main score card (CRL Rank).
  • Candidates appearing in final 10+2 examinations are also eligible to apply provisionally.

For B.Arch Courses

  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed Class 12 with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.
  • Minimum Aggregate Marks: At least 50% aggregate marks in 10+2 standard.
  • Compulsory Scorecard: Must possess a valid score in National Aptitude Test in Architecture (NATA) or JEE Main Paper 2.

MP DTE Seat Reservation & Quota Rules

Understand the reservation percentages applicable to candidates belonging to Madhya Pradesh.

The Directorate of Technical Education enforces reservation rules for MP Domicile candidates in government and private institutions. Reserved seats are allocated to candidates who upload valid caste and income certificates verified by competent authorities.

Category-Wise Seat Allocation Percentage

Scheduled Tribes (ST) 20%
Scheduled Castes (SC) 16%
Other Backward Classes (OBC-NCL) 14%
Economically Weaker Section (EWS) 10%
Unreserved / General (UR) 40%
Horizontal Reservation Policies
Within each vertical category, horizontal reservations are applied: 30% seats are reserved for female candidates, 3% for Persons with Disability (PwD), and specific quotas for Children of Defense Personnel / Freedom Fighters.

MP DTE Counselling 2026: Step-by-Step Process

Follow this step-by-step walkthrough of the online DTE counselling cycle to secure your admission.

1

Online Registration & Profile Creation

Candidates register on the official MP DTE portal using their JEE Main application number, name, and contact details. A unique registration ID is generated for future logins.

2

Document Uploading & Online Verification

Upload scanned copies of required documents (Marksheets, Category, Domicile). DTE verification centers check the documents online. Any discrepancy is notified through SMS/Email.

3

Choice Filling & Priority Selection

Log in to select your choice of engineering branches and institutions in order of your priority. You can choose any number of options. Research previous cutoff ranks before finalizing.

4

Choice Locking & Fee Payment

After arranging, pay the counselling fee (typically Rs 1530/- including portal fee) and lock your choices. Once locked, choices cannot be changed. Print your locked choice receipt.

5

Common Merit List Declaration

DTE MP releases a common merit list based on JEE Main CRL Rank and qualifying details. Candidates can raise objections if their rank is not mapped properly.

6

Seat Allotment & Reporting to College

Based on merit and choices locked, a seat allotment letter is issued. Candidates must report to the allocated college within the deadline, verify originals, and pay the tuition fee to confirm.

Document Upload Advisory: Avoid Common Rejections

• Resolution: Documents must be scanned clearly. Blurred mobile camera pictures are often rejected.

• Verification Authorities: Category and domicile certificates must be issued by MP authorized digital portals (District/Tehsil levels).

• Name Matching: The spellings of your name and parents' names must exactly match across Class 10/12 marksheets and Category certificates.

• File Formats: Documents must be in PDF format with sizes between 200KB and 500KB.

Expert Choice Filling Tips & Strategy

Choice filling determines your branch and college. Make smart decisions based on past trends.

Analyze Cutoff Trends

Check the previous year cutoff opening and closing ranks for your category. Arrange choices matching your rank zone. Don't rely solely on the first round cutoffs; check final round data too.

Priority Order Matters

Arrange choices in strict decreasing order of your personal preference. Place dream choices at the top, realistic choices in the middle, and guaranteed safety backup choices at the bottom.

Fill Ample Choices

Do not restrict yourself to only 5 or 10 choices. Fill maximum options of branches and colleges you are willing to join to prevent "No Seat Allotted" status in Round 1.

Choice Locking

Verify options, double-check orders, and always manually Lock your choices by paying the portal fee. Unlocked choices will be auto-locked at the deadline but manual locking guarantees security.
