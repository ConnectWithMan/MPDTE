Overview of the Counselling Process

The MP DTE Counselling Process is a completely online procedure that facilitates admissions into various technical courses like B.E./B.Tech and B.Arch in Madhya Pradesh. Candidates must participate in this centralized counselling based on their JEE Main merit.

The process consists of multiple rounds, each involving specific steps that must be completed within stipulated deadlines. Below is a detailed breakdown of each phase of the counselling process.

Eligibility Criteria for MP DTE B.Tech Admission

Make sure you meet the following baseline requirements before initiating your registration on the DTE MP portal.

🎓

Academic Qualification

Passed 10+2 (Class 12) or equivalent exam with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject.

Min. 45% (40% for SC/ST/OBC)
📝

JEE Main Rank

Round 1 of counselling is exclusively conducted based on JEE Main CRL (Common Rank List) ranks. Non-JEE candidates are eligible to apply in later rounds based on Class 12 marks.

JEE Main Score Required
🏠

Domicile Rules

90% of the total seats in Government and Government-aided colleges are reserved for candidates holding MP Domicile. 10% seats are open for Outside Madhya Pradesh (All India) candidates.

Step 1: Online Registration

Registration is the mandatory first step to participate in the counselling. It is a fully online process where you establish your candidature.

  • Official Portal Access: Visit the official DTE MP counselling portal (dte.mponline.gov.in) and navigate to the B.Tech section.
  • Verification of JEE Details: Register using your JEE Main Roll Number and Date of Birth. The portal will automatically pull your JEE scores and rank.
  • Personal & Domicile Details: Fill in your personal information, address, and select your domicile status (MP Domicile vs. All India). Domicile status cannot be changed after registration closes.
  • Academic Information: Enter details of your Class 10th (for DOB proof) and Class 12th examinations (marks/grades).
  • Category & Benefit Options: Declare your category (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) and choose whether you wish to apply under sub-categories like Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW), Freedom Fighter (FF), Soldier (S), or Physically Handicapped (PH).
  • Bank Account Details: Provide active bank account details (account holder name, account number, IFSC code). This is crucial for receiving refunds in case of seat cancellation or withdrawal.
  • Document Uploads: Upload a recent passport-sized photograph and your signature in the prescribed format.
  • Counselling Fee: Pay the non-refundable registration and counselling fee online using Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI (approx. Rs. 2,800/- for general/unreserved categories).
⚠️ CRITICAL REGISTRATION WARNING
Use a mobile number and email address that are active and easily accessible to you. DTE MP will send all vital OTPs (for registration and choice locking) and final seat allotment notifications to this mobile number. You cannot change this number later in the counselling cycle.

Step 2: Document Verification

After registration, the uploaded certificates are cross-checked by the DTE verification officers to confirm eligibility.

  • Automatic & Online Verification: Most candidates have their documents verified completely online. The portal cross-references details with Madhya Pradesh state database (like caste and domicile status).
  • Verification Receipt: Once approved, a digital "Verification Slip" is generated in your candidate login dashboard. Download this slip, as it is a required document for final reporting.
  • Nodal Center Visits: If the online officer finds a discrepancy (e.g. illegible certificate, mismatch in name spelling, missing documents), your verification status will change to "Objection Raised." You will need to immediately resolve the issue online or visit a government helpline nodal center physically.

⚠️ THE "OBJECTION" ALERT
Check your candidate portal daily! If an objection is raised on any uploaded document, you must upload a corrected version or submit a correction grievance within 24 to 48 hours. Failing to resolve an objection in time will lead to candidate disqualification from that round.

Step 3: Choice Filling and Locking

This is the most critical step in the entire DTE counselling. Your final seat allotment depends heavily on how wisely you order and lock your college preferences.

  • Accessing the Choice Dashboard: Log in to the MP DTE portal after your online verification is complete. Navigate to "Choice Filling."
  • Exhaustive Choices: You will see a list of all participating government, government-aided, and private engineering colleges. Select as many choices as possible. Selecting only 2 or 3 choices increases the risk of receiving zero allotments.
  • Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) Option: If you are an MP domicile candidate with family income under Rs. 8 Lakhs per annum and opted for TFW during registration, you can select TFW seats (e.g. `SGSITS Indore - CS (TFW)`). These seats charge Rs. 0 tuition fee! They should be placed high on your priority list.
  • Prioritization Strategy: Arrange your choices in strict decreasing order of your personal preference. Place your dream branches/colleges at the top, followed by realistic options, and fallback options at the bottom. The allotment system evaluates choices from top to bottom.
  • Saving & Previewing: Regularly click "Save Choices" to prevent session timeouts. Thoroughly review the final order in the preview panel before proceeding.
  • Final Choice Locking: Lock your choices before the round's deadline. Choice locking requires verification via a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number.
💡 GOLDEN RULE OF CHOICE FILLING
"Put what you want first, not what you think you will get." The computer system checks your options starting from Option 1 downwards. If you qualify for Option 1, you get it. If you put a lower preferred college at Option 1 just because you expect to get it, the system will allocate it to you immediately, and your higher preferences will be ignored!
⚠️ WARNING: LOCKING IS ABSOLUTE
Once choices are locked and the OTP is submitted, they cannot be edited, reordered, or deleted under any circumstances. If you forget to lock your choices, the system will auto-lock your last saved choices when the deadline expires.

Step 4: Seat Allotment

At the end of the choice-locking period, DTE runs the automated allocation algorithm based on candidate ranks, locked preferences, and seat reservations.

  • Allotment Result Declaration: DTE MP publishes the seat allotment list on the portal. Candidates must log in using their credentials to view their individual seat allotment status.
  • Downloading Allotment Letter: If you are allocated a seat, you can download your provisional Allotment Letter. This document contains the name of the allotted college, branch, and category of allotment.
  • Seat Acceptance Fee: To reserve the seat, candidates must pay a partial tuition fee (typically Rs. 10,000/- or as specified by DTE) online via the DTE portal. Failure to pay this fee within the deadline will result in immediate cancellation of the seat allotment, and you will be barred from further normal rounds.
  • Choice Decisions: After paying the fee, you must choose one of the following paths:
    • Freeze: You are satisfied with the current seat. You accept it and will report to the college to secure admission.
    • Upward Movement (Float): You accept the current seat as a security, but want to participate in the next round for a chance to upgrade to one of your higher-priority choices. If upgraded, the current seat is automatically released; if not, the current seat is preserved.
💡 HOW UPWARD MOVEMENT SAVES SEATS
If you opt for Upward Movement, you do NOT lose your current seat. The system holds your seat. In the next round, if a seat in your higher preferences becomes available, you get the new seat and your old seat is given to someone else. If no upgrade is possible, you keep your original seat.

Step 5: Reporting to the Allotted Institute

To finalize your admission, you must report physically to the allotted college. If you fail to report within the specified dates, your allotment will be cancelled automatically.

  • Report within Deadline: DTE provides a window of 4–5 working days for reporting. Plan your travel accordingly. Check the college's operational hours.
  • Physical Verification: The admission committee of the allotted college will physically inspect all your original documents and compare them with the online verification slip.
  • Fee Clearance: Pay the remaining first-semester tuition fee directly at the college counter (through online transfer, bank challan, or Demand Draft as specified by the college).
  • Admission Confirmation: The college will issue an official Admission Confirmation Letter. Keep this letter safe; it confirms your enrolment.

MP DTE Counselling Rounds Overview

The MP DTE B.Tech counselling is conducted in three main sequential rounds to fill seats across various institutes.

Round Admission Basis Target Candidates Allotment Quota
Round 1 (First Phase) JEE Main Score / CRL Rank MP State Domicile & Outside MP (All India) candidates. Home State Quota (90%) + All India Quota (10%).
Round 2 (Second Phase) JEE Main Ranks / Class 12 Marks Candidates who missed Round 1 or want to upgrade their seats, plus non-JEE candidates. Vacant seats after Round 1. JEE rank candidates get higher priority than Class 12 candidates.
CLC Round (College Level) Class 12 Marks / Merit List Direct physical reporting at respective colleges. Open to all unregistered/vacant candidates. Final vacant seats at the institutional level. Spot admission.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Is physical presence required for document verification?

Generally, document verification is conducted online. However, if any issues arise with the uploaded documents, candidates may be directed to visit a nodal center.

2. What happens if I forget to lock my choices?

If you forget to lock your choices manually, the system will automatically lock your last saved choices after the deadline expires.

3. Can I change my choices after locking them?

No, once the choices are locked, they cannot be modified. Make sure to review them carefully before locking.

4. What is the Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme and who is eligible?

The TFW scheme is an MP government initiative that waives 100% of the tuition fee for eligible students. To qualify, you must be a resident of Madhya Pradesh, your total family income must be less than Rs. 8 Lakhs per annum, and you must apply for TFW during registration.

5. Can I participate in Round 2 if I am satisfied with my Round 1 seat?

If you are satisfied, you should "Freeze" the seat and complete the admission. If you participate in Round 2 and get allocated a new seat, your Round 1 seat will be cancelled automatically, even if you like the first seat better. Only participate in Round 2 if you want an upgrade.

6. How does College Level Counselling (CLC) round work?

CLC is the final spot admission phase conducted directly at the individual college campus. Candidates must physically report to the college of their choice with all original documents and fees. Seats are offered on the spot based on the merit list of candidates present on that specific day.
